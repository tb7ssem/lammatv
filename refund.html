Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to all orders from LAMMA TV

1. Digital products (IPTV subscriptions)

IPTV activations are delivered fast, so our guarantee is practical:

  • Free trial first. We offer a 24-hour free trial so you can test the service before paying — so "it doesn't work" complaints on paid plans are handled from a working baseline.
  • 24-hour fixing window. If your paid activation fails on your device, tell us on WhatsApp within 24 hours. We fix it or re-issue codes at no cost.
  • Full refund within 48 hours if we cannot make the service work on your compatible device after you've reported the issue and cooperated with our setup help.
  • No refunds after 72 hours of successful activation, or for change of mind, missing channels from third-party lineup changes, or unstable internet on your side. Outage credits replace refunds when the issue is on our servers.

2. Physical products (electronics, boxes, accessories)

  • Inspect your order at delivery — broken-on-arrival items are replaced free.
  • Returns accepted within 14 days with the item in its original packaging and accessories, evidenced by the WhatsApp receipt.
  • Refunds are issued after we receive and check the item, or as store credit if you prefer.
  • Return shipping is covered by us only when the item is defective; for change-of-mind returns, shipping is on the buyer.

3. How to request a refund

Message us on WhatsApp (+216 58 877 141) with your order reference (LT-… / WA-…) and a short description, ideally a screen recording of the issue. We answer within a few hours during working hours.
