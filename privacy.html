Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What we collect

  • Order details you type (name, phone, address for shipped items, notes) — they are formatted into a WhatsApp message and processed by us on WhatsApp to fulfill your order.
  • Browser storage on your device (shopping bag, language of choice) — kept locally in your browser to make the site work; it never leaves your device except for the order message you send yourself.
  • We do not run advertising trackers, sell data, or send marketing without your consent. No payment card data is collected on this website.

2. WhatsApp

Ordering via WhatsApp means your name/phone and the message content are also handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms, processed by Meta. Keep that in mind when sharing details there.

3. Your rights

You can ask us any time to delete your order records — just message us from your order phone number and mention your order reference. We will do it within 7 days, except where laws require us to keep a sales record.

4. Hosting

The site itself is served as static files by GitHub Pages (no accounts, no server-side profiles created). Product data is fetched on each visit.
